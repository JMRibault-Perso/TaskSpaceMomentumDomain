Safety-critical

Safety-critical V-model design, development, documentation & testing

Traceable engineering for systems where failure is unacceptable. Requirements decompose down the left side of the V; each level is verified on the right side, and the trace between them is maintained as the design changes.

R&D through commercialization

Research → design → deploy → commercialize

We don't stop at a slide deck or a lab demo. Engagements reduce risk early, prove value in the task space, and leave you with something operable: a product, a service, or an integrated capability.

  1. Frame the mission, constraints, and success metrics, and decide whether to build, buy, integrate, or wait.
  2. Research & develop the hard parts (perception, planning, controls, data, interfaces) with clear kill criteria.
  3. Design for operations: maintainability, safety, and the people who will run it.
  4. Deploy forward: on site when it matters, remote when that is enough.
  5. Commercialize & sustain as a product, a RaaS offering, or an internal capability.

Working principles

  • Physical-world honesty. Bench numbers that don't hold in the real cell don't count.
  • Integration first. Interfaces, operators, and QA are design inputs, not afterthoughts.
  • Clear commercial shape. Know what you're selling or operating before you scale.
  • No theater. A smaller system that works beats a broad vision that doesn't.
