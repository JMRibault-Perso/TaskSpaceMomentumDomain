Robotics & Automation Accelerator

The Taskspace Momentum Accelerator

A structured fast track from use case to a working robotic cell. We specialize in rapid integration of commercial off-the-shelf (COTS) robotics for your application, combining proven arms, grippers, and cameras with Physical AI and computer vision, custom fixtures, and built-in verification & validation. You see your task running on real hardware before you commit to scale.

  1. Use caseDefine the task, constraints, safety needs, and what success looks like.
  2. COTS selectionChoose the arm, gripper, cameras, and controller for the task. Vendor-agnostic.
  3. IntegratePhysical AI / CV perception, motion, and custom fixtures for your parts or labware.
  4. VerifyHIL bench and test plan with traceability, so issues show up on the bench.
  5. Decide & scaleHand over the cell, the evidence, and a path forward: in-house, product, or RaaS.

Need something working before a product ships? The accelerator applies the same building blocks to your application with COTS robots today. See Services for the full picture.
