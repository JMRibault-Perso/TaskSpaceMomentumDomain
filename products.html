AI-enabled robot integration hub

An AI-enabled hub for integrating off-the-shelf robots into your application. It brings arms, grippers, cameras, and sensors onto one integration layer, and uses sensor fusion to drive AI-in-the-loop automated training, so behaviors are trained and checked against real sensor data instead of hand-tuned for every new robot.

  • Off-the-shelf robot integrationArms, grippers, and controllers from different vendors, connected through one hub.
  • Sensor fusionCameras, depth, force/torque, and robot state combined into one consistent view of the task space.
  • AI-in-the-loop automated trainingFused, real-world data feeds training and retraining of Physical AI behaviors.
  • Verification hooksHIL, SIL, and MIL test points so every change is verified before it reaches the cell.

The Taskspace Momentum Accelerator

A structured fast track from use case to a working robotic cell. We specialize in rapid integration of commercial off-the-shelf (COTS) robotics for your application, combining proven arms, grippers, and cameras with Physical AI and computer vision, custom fixtures, and built-in verification & validation. You see your task running on real hardware before you commit to scale.

  1. Use caseDefine the task, constraints, safety needs, and what success looks like.
