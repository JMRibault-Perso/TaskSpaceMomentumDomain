What we do

Five ways we get robots doing real work

Each theme stands on its own, and they work best together: a trained robot, the parts that make it deployable, the platform that runs it, the views that guide it, and the expert know-how that teaches it.

01

Physical AI

AI that perceives, decides and acts in the real world. Bring your own robot, tell us the task, and we'll train it quickly for lab automation, test fixtures and repetitive bench work.

  • Your robot or an off-the-shelf arm
  • Trained for your task, not a demo
  • Retrained as the work changes
02

Robotics enablers

The building blocks that make a robot deployable: sensor fusion, an AI-enabled integration hub, live digital twins kept in sync through perception, and rapid prototyping with in-house 3D printing.

  • Fixtures, mounts and harnesses for your cell
  • Camera, lighting and calibration setups
  • Digital twin modeling and MBSE simulation
03

Forward-deployed engineering platform

A forward-deployed engineering platform we bring to your project, from the cloud to the cell: models, data and fleet configuration in the cloud, plus on-site runtime services that supervise each robot. Engineers work alongside your team on site, and every change is versioned and traceable.

  • Deployed on your site and hardware
  • Engineers embedded with your team
  • Versioned, traceable changes
04

AR/VR integrations

See and steer your system in context. Overlay digital twins on the real cell, rehearse tasks in VR before touching hardware, and guide operators hands-free.

  • Guided setup, maintenance and inspection
  • Simulation and test results in context
  • Rehearse in VR, then run on the real cell
05

User capture with AI integration

Capture how experts actually work, from motion and video to voice and input, and turn it into training data and AI assistance for robots and people.

  • Motion, video, voice and input capture
  • Training data for robots
  • AI assistance for people on the job

How we work

Three steps, short loops

Every engagement follows the same loop. Changes are tried in the model first and proven on hardware as soon as they are built.

  1. ScopeWe scope the task with you: the work, the constraints and what success looks like.
  2. Prototype & trainWe prototype and train quickly, on a digital twin first and then on real hardware.
  3. Deploy & improveWe deploy in the field, measure the results and keep improving, or hand it over to your team.
